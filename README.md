# shot idea

**Your ideas, one app. Make your life easy.**

shot idea is a free Windows app to capture your screen, mark it up, copy and translate text,
record video and present live in meetings, all in one place. No account, no ads, no tracking: your files stay on your PC.

## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Screenshot** | Print Screen, drag an area, copy or save. Or right-click any picture and choose *Edit with shot idea* |
| **Mark up** | Arrows, boxes, pen, highlighter, numbered steps, text, icons, zoom lens, blur private details |
| **Copy & translate** | Copy text from any picture (English, Thai and more) and see the translation side by side |
| **Record video** | Record an area or the whole screen as MP4, with your voice, PC sound and a camera bubble |
| **Present live** | Spotlight, quick marks, zoom, notes and your camera on screen while you share in Teams, Google Meet or Zoom |
| **PDF & Print** | Save a screenshot as a PDF, or print it with preview: portrait or landscape, any printer, fit to page |
| **Cloud** | Save screenshots, PDFs and videos to your own OneDrive or Google Drive (optional) |
| **Clipchamp** | Edit your recordings in Clipchamp: trim, add titles or music |

## New in version 1.1

- Save as PDF and Print with preview (portrait or landscape, any printer, fit picture to page; Ctrl + P)
- OneDrive or Google Drive: save there too, and open your pictures from the start bar
- Edit in Clipchamp after a recording
- shot idea Hand handwriting font (English, German, Thai), or any font on your PC
- Setting in tabs, and a new guided tour that also shows where shot idea is in the tray
- 32-bit (x86) Windows supported as well as 64-bit
