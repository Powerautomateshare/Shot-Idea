# shot idea brand guidelines

"shot idea" and the shot idea logo (orange corner brackets on a midnight-navy square) are the
name and logo of the shot idea app, created and owned by **Sorasit Moungon**.

These guidelines explain how you may use the name and logo. They work together with the
[licence](LICENSE.txt): the licence covers using the app, these guidelines cover using the brand.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name **shot idea** and the logo to point people to the official app, for example in
  articles, reviews, blog posts, videos, tutorials, school or work guides.
- Link to the official app with the logo or a "Get shot idea" button, as long as the link goes to
  the official Microsoft Store page or to this GitHub page.
- Show real screenshots of shot idea.

When you do, please:

- Use the logo exactly as provided: no new colours, no stretching, no rotating, no effects,
  and no extra words or images on top of it.
- Write the name as **shot idea** (all lower case).
- Make it clear that shot idea is made by Sorasit Moungon, not by you.

## You need written permission to

- Put the name or logo on your own product, app, website, service or company name.
- Put the name or logo on merchandise (T-shirts, stickers, mugs and so on).
- Use the name or logo in advertising for anything other than shot idea itself.
- Use the name or logo for an event, course or training you charge for.

## You may not

- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests Sorasit Moungon made, supports, partners with or
  approves your product, service or content when he doesn't.
- Use a name or logo that is easy to confuse with shot idea, for example "Shot Idea Pro",
  "ShotIdeaX", "Shot-Idea Plus", or a look-alike corner-bracket logo for a screen-capture app.
- Sell anything under the shot idea name or logo, or publish copies of the app
  (the [licence](LICENSE.txt) allows only the author to sell or distribute shot idea).
- Use the name or logo in anything misleading, unlawful, hateful or harmful.
- Register "shot idea" or a similar name as a domain, social media account, app-store listing
  or trademark.

## Other companies' brands

shot idea works with apps such as Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and Zoom, and is published on the
Microsoft Store and GitHub. Those names and logos belong to their owners. Mentioning them describes
where shot idea runs or what it works with; it does not mean those companies made, support or
approve shot idea.

## Asking for permission or reporting misuse

Open an issue on this page:
[github.com/Powerautomateshare/Shot-Idea/issues](https://github.com/Powerautomateshare/Shot-Idea/issues)
(or use **Send feedback** in the app's About window). Please say what you'd like to do, or where you
saw the name or logo used.

Sorasit Moungon may update these guidelines at any time, and may ask anyone to stop a use of the
name or logo that doesn't follow them.
